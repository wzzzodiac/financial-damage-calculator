# Financial Damage Calculator

A small browser calculator that translates a purchase into work hours, disposable-income impact, Döner equivalents and a deliberately dramatic financial-damage score.

## Inputs

- Purchase price
- Hourly net income
- Monthly working hours
- Monthly fixed expenses
- Current debt
- Average Döner price

## Results

- Estimated work hours required for the purchase
- Monthly net income
- Disposable monthly income
- Percentage of monthly income consumed
- Percentage of disposable income consumed
- Days of disposable income represented by the purchase
- Döner equivalent
- Debt opportunity-cost comparison
- 0–100 entertainment-oriented damage score
- Share / clipboard result output

## Important note

This project is an entertainment and rough-comparison tool. The scoring formula is intentionally simplified and is **not financial advice**.

## Tech

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Results are calculated entirely in the browser; there is no backend and no financial data is transmitted anywhere.
